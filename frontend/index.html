Hostel Information & Facilities

Confirmed campus arrangements at Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan's S.P.I.T. Campus, Andheri West.

Verified Campus Facilities

Available to registered hostel residents across residential blocks:

  • WiFi Connectivity: High-speed campus-wide network access for academic work.
  • Solar Water Heaters: Rooftop solar water heating systems installed for hot water supply.
  • Drinking Water: Multi-stage RO filtration water coolers on every residential floor.
  • Study Hall: Dedicated quiet study rooms accessible for evening reference and exam preparation.
  • Gated Security: 24x7 security personnel and CCTV surveillance at campus and hostel access gates.

Gate Timings & Curfew Policy

Standard gate operation hours established by the hostel warden:

  • Morning Gate Opening: 06:00 AM daily
  • Weekday Curfew: 10:00 PM (Monday to Friday)
  • Weekend Curfew: 10:30 PM (Saturday and Sunday)
  • Night & Outstation Passes: Must be requested through Hostel Ops at least 24 hours prior.
Institutional Notice: Hostel capacity, availability and mess plans are managed directly by the SPIT administration.
